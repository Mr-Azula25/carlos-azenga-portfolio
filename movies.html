My Top 5 Screen Stories

These are five of my favourite anime and television series. I enjoy their storytelling, memorable characters and suspense.

  2. Death Note (2006) — The battle of intelligence and the moral questions make it gripping.
  3. The Originals (2013) — I enjoy the supernatural world, family relationships and dramatic twists.
  4. Money Heist (2017) — The planning, teamwork and unexpected turns make it exciting to watch.
  5. Breaking Bad (2008) — Its character development and consequences of choices make it memorable.
